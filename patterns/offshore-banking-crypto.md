# Offshore Banking in Crypto

The "offshore banking" metaphor in crypto refers to protocols that enable users to hold and transfer synthetic fiat assets (especially USD) outside traditional banking systems, with strong privacy guarantees. The goal is to replicate the function of offshore bank accounts (privacy, asset protection, currency diversification) using decentralised technology.

## Implementations

- **[[projects/haven]]:** Markets itself explicitly as a decentralised offshore bank. Users mint xUSD (and other xAssets) by locking XHV collateral, then transfer xUSD privately. The protocol supports xEUR, xGBP, xBTC, xAU (gold), etc.

## Relevance to Logos

The offshore banking use case highlights demand for private stable value storage and transfer. For LEZ, a privacy-preserving cross-chain DEX could serve a similar function: users swap into private stable assets and hold them in a shielded state. Key requirements: deep liquidity (Haven's weakness), reliable pegs, and regulatory resilience.
