Baltex Announces XMR to TRX Swaps Now Completing in as Little as 20 Minutes

Jacó, Costa Rica, March 2nd, 2026, Chainwire


Baltex.io, the one of the leading non-custodial, privacy-first cryptocurrency swap platform, is proud to highlight the exceptional performance of its Monero (XMR) to TRON (TRX) swaps, with many transactions now finalizing in approximately 20 minutes or less under optimal conditions.

Baltex empowers users to seamlessly convert privacy-focused assets like Monero into high-speed, low-cost networks such as TRON, all without registration, KYC requirements, or compromising on anonymity. By leveraging aggregated liquidity from top centralized and decentralized exchanges (CEX + DEX), combined with its innovative Monero-based private routing technology, Baltex delivers fast, secure, and truly private cross-chain swaps.

“Privacy enthusiasts and everyday crypto users alike need reliable ways to move value between chains without leaving traces or facing unnecessary delays,” said Andri Ko, CMO at Baltex. “Our XMR to TRX swaps exemplify how we’ve optimized routing for speed while preserving Monero’s core privacy features. In many cases, users receive their TRX in around 20 minutes after deposit confirmations — a significant advantage in today’s fast-moving market.”

The process remains straightforward and user-friendly. Users can:

  1. Visit Baltex.io
  2. Select the XMR to TRX pair and enter the desired amount
  3. Review the live rate, network fees, and any service charges
  4. Send XMR to the provided one-time deposit address
  5. After the required network confirmations and optimal routing, receive TRX directly in your wallet

Baltex supports over 10,000 tokens across more than 200 networks, including major chains like Ethereum, Solana, BSC, Polygon, Avalanche, and Tron. Its Private Swap mode — powered by Monero rails — inserts shielded hops to break on-chain linkability, protecting users from surveillance, community tracking, or chain analysis. This can make it ideal for high-volume traders, OTC deals, and anyone prioritizing financial confidentiality.

Rated 4.3 stars on TrustPilot, Baltex continues to gain recognition for its clean interface, reliable execution, and commitment to user sovereignty. The platform’s infrastructure has been audited for security, ensuring peace of mind for privacy-conscious individuals and Web3 participants.

As regulatory scrutiny increases and on-chain transparency becomes a growing concern, Baltex stands out by offering practical tools for maintaining economic freedom. The enhanced XMR to TRX capability reinforces Baltex’s position as the go-to platform for private, efficient cross-chain swaps.

More information available at https://baltex.io or the XMR to TRX page directly at https://baltex.io/currencies/xmr-to-trx.
