Baltex Launches Fully Cross-Chain Private Swaps

Press Release
HomeResearchBaltex Launches Fully Cross-Chain Private Swaps
By
Partner Content
24 November 2025 at 13:55
Share

Copy link

Jaco, Costa Rica, November 24th, 2025, Chainwire

Baltex, a leading non-custodial cryptocurrency swap platform, today announced the launch of Private Swaps - a groundbreaking feature that delivers true on-chain privacy.

While most blockchains are transparent by design, Private Swaps completely breaks the link between deposit and payout addresses using Monero as an intermediate privacy layer. 

“In today’s world, demand for truly private transactions is growing exponentially - whether to protect personal financial data, shield business deals from competitors, or simply preserve basic economic freedom,” said Andrei K., CMO of Baltex.io. “Private Swaps solves this problem once and for all: we bring extended privacy to crypto without forcing users to compromise on speed, cost, or convenience.”

How Private Swaps Work

  1. User selects any trading pair and chooses “Private” mode.
  2. Baltex.io generates a one-time deposit address.
  3. Incoming funds are instantly routed through Monero’s privacy protocol.
  4. The desired asset is delivered to the user’s wallet with zero traceable connection to the original deposit.

Average completion time: 10-20 minutes, depending on Monero network congestion.

Fees remain among the lowest in the industry - starting at just 0.4%.

Users retain full control at every step. The platform stays 100 % non-custodial, never touching or holding customer funds.

Private Swaps run parallel to the existing “Efficient” mode, giving traders the freedom to choose between maximum speed and maximum privacy on a per-transaction basis. Over 1,000 supported assets, fixed-rate options make Baltex.io one of the most flexible swap services on the market.

The launch comes at a time when regulatory pressure and on-chain surveillance are at all-time highs. High-net-worth individuals, privacy-focused funds, OTC desks, and everyday users now have a reliable, battle-tested tool to protect their financial sovereignty.

To start using Private Swaps, users can visit baltex.io.

About Baltex.io

Baltex is a non-custodial instant swap platform supporting 1,000+ cryptocurrencies across all major blockchains. Built for speed, transparent fees, and now unbreakable privacy, Baltex.io is trusted by users worldwide.
