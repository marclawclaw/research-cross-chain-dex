Terms of Service Review

Eigenwallet is a privacy-first, open-source wallet offering self-custody and peer-to-peer atomic swaps between BTC and XMR, with built-in Tor for network anonymity.

Atomic Swaps

Supports peer-to-peer exchange between Bitcoin and Monero with no accounts required and zero risk of frozen funds.

Self-Custody

Users have complete control of their Monero funds, enforcing a strict your keys, your coins policy.

Tor Integration

Includes built-in Tor integration to ensure your network traffic remains completely anonymous and untraceable.

Open Source

The wallet's code is strictly transparent and auditable, as it is an open source project built by the community.
