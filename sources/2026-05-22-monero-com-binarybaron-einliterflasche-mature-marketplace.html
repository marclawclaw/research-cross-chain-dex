binarybaron and einliterflasche submit CCS proposal to 'transform' XMR-BTC atomic swaps ecosystem into 'mature marketplace'

Posted Tue, 09 Jul 2024, from Monero Observer

Source Article Link: https://monero.observer/binarybaron-einliterflasche-ccs-proposal-transform-xmr-btc-atomic-swaps-mature-marketplace/

binarybaron1 and einliterflashche2 have submitted a CCS proposal3 looking to continue XMR-BTC atomic swaps4 development and help transform the ecosystem from a prototype into a mature marketplace:

We are confident that given a few months time to fully dedicate to the project, we can transform the ecosystem from more of a proof of concept into a mature marketplace. We are asking the community for funding in order to be able to focus our efforts on improving the atomic swaps ecosystem.

Total funding: 498 XMR.

ETA: 6 months.

Tasks overview

  • continue to maintain both the GUI and the underlying CLI
  • CLI: focus will be to improve overall reliability
  • GUI: make it more accessible to non technical users
  • GUI: release an Android (and possibly iOS) version
  • continue to manage GUI webpage and docs, public registry of swap providers [..]

binarybaron and einliterflashche developed unstoppableswap-gui5, which facilitated more than 3,000 mainnet swaps in 2023, and are the current6 maintainers of the xmr-btc-swap project.

To learn more about binarybaron’s work, consult previous Monero Observer reports7’8’9.

To read, share your feedback, ask questions and support this proposal, consult !4773.

This is an ongoing story and the report will be updated when new information is available.
